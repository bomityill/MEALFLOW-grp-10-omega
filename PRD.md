
PROBLEM OBSERVED
    after saturday church services, students experience long queues at the cafeteria while waiting to scan their id cards and collect their meals, since students come to the same point to collect food from different churches, the process becomes congested and students would have to wait under the sun before it gets to their turn for their ids to be scanned.

THOSE THE PROBLEM AFFECTS
    this problem affects mostly students as after a stressful church service they would still come to queue for food. take for example those whose church parish is in the BUTH 600 seater which is really far from the school's cafeteria. after finishing servivce they would still have to walk down to queue. 

 HOW MOST STUDENTS COPE
    most students take umbrella to church knowing well they'll wait in the scorching sun after church or some carry along a fan with them because of the heat, some others try to leave church earlier in order to beat the crowd,some wait for the crowd to reduce but that could take a lot of time. lets take for example a student amanda. Amanda leaves church after a 2hr 30 min service and knows she still has to get food from cafeteria because all the other shops are closed because of the sabbath observance. she arrives at the cafeteria and sees a large crowd of students already waiting to scan their IDs to collect food. Amanda joins the queue waiting for her turn, she's under the scorching sun waiting and the only thing to help her is her umbrella and fan, she gets tired and begins to feel frustrated but she has no alternative but to wait because she needs to collect her food. After few minutes she gets to the front scans her card and collects her food and leaves feeling hot and tired. This is why our solution would be helpul.

  SOLUTION
  the solution we're proposing is to decentralise the collection of food i.e not allowing food collection to be from one single point.this can be acheived be distributing meals to designated church centres before church services finish. there would be an app for the coordination of this process, thhis proposed app would generate a qr code for students which the cafeteria personnel would scan to verify their eligibility so they could collect their food and go straight to their hall

  MVP
  DEFINING THE MVP
  this app will consist of features that enables students to access a digital meal card or ticket through a qr code which the app would generate once a student inputs their information. the food distributing personnel (caf workers) would then use a scanning interface to verify student eligibility and record successful meal collection in each worship centres.
    the first version of this proposed app would focus on student authentication, worship centre information, qr based meal verification, meal collection tracking, and basic meal allocation. these few features are sufficient to check whether a decentralised meal distribution system can help reduce waiting times, minimise the crowd in caf and improve the overalL food collection for students. there could also be a feature where students cvanm input the type of rice they want to eat the nigh before so that cafeteria workers can know the amount of type of food to deploy to these various worship centres.
  
  WHAT THIS MVP WOULD REQUIRE
  A LOGIN AND AUTHENTICATION SYSTEM TO VERIFY STUDENTS ELIGIBILITY
  A QR CODE SCANNING AND VERIFICATION
  A GENERAL DASHBOARD
  AN INTERATICVE INTERFACE

     WHAT IS THE GOAL OF THIS MVP
     to provide students with faster and more convenint way of collecting food after saturday church service by moving food collection points to designated church centresand using a digital system to verify student's IDs and record meal collection.
